\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents a drone-side voice safety component for emergency interruption under severe self-noise. The design narrows speech to emergency-oriented intent events and routes every event through an auditable control bridge. This framing is narrower than general ASR and more cautious than direct speech-to-flight control.

The recognizer combines noise-aware training with a publishable split between an anchor recognizer and an embedded student. The ESP32 prototype shows how the embedded student can become a local voice-event source for a command/state log. The remaining work is to complete labeled acoustic validation, gate false-trigger evaluation, and no-propeller control-bridge logging so that the system-level safety claim rests on bridge behavior rather than classifier output alone.
