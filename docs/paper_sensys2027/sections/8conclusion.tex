\section{Conclusion}

This draft reframes the project around the part that is already technically coherent: on-device UAV intent recognition under rotor noise, with emergency-aware evaluation and a training pipeline built on noisy augmentation and clean-to-noisy transfer. The current benchmark evidence is strong enough to support that story. The next milestone is to complete the systems evidence that SenSys reviewers will expect, especially onboard latency, real-world protocol-driven tests, and safety-oriented false-trigger analysis.
