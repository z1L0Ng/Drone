\title{\sys: On-Device Emergency Intent Recognition Under Extreme Rotor Noise}

\begin{abstract}
Small UAVs need hands-free interaction, but onboard voice control breaks down when rotor noise overlaps with safety-critical speech. This paper drafts \sys, an on-device audio-to-intent pipeline for three command classes: \emph{emergency}, \emph{movement}, and \emph{unknown}. The current manuscript centers on a deployment-oriented training stack rather than a generic speech benchmark: direct intent prediction, class-aware noisy augmentation, and teacher-student transfer from clean to noisy conditions. On the current balanced noisy benchmark at evaluation SNR $=-10$ dB, our best completed configuration improves overall accuracy from $0.72$ to $0.88$ and emergency recall from $0.47$ to $0.79$ over a direct noisy-training baseline. This version reorganizes the paper around the claims that are already supported, while isolating still-missing evidence such as onboard latency and real-flight validation for the next writing round.
\end{abstract}
