\title{\sys: Voice-Driven UAV Safety Interaction Under Rotor Noise}

\begin{abstract}
Speaking to a nearby drone is a natural interaction mode, especially when a person cannot quickly reach a controller, phone application, or networked interface. However, rotor self-noise and flight-control risk make direct speech-to-command execution unsafe. This paper introduces \sys, an onboard voice safety layer for small UAVs. \sys{} converts short audio windows into logged intent events before any control policy considers action. The event space is deliberately narrow: movement represents ordinary control-oriented speech, emergency represents safety-critical speech, and unknown captures unsupported or uncertain audio. This design lets the system treat speech as evidence for a conservative control boundary rather than as a direct flight command. We implement \sys{} with a noise-aware recognizer and an ESP32 runtime path that performs local audio capture, feature extraction, integer inference, and event reporting.
\end{abstract}
